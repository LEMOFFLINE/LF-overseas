# SEO rollout

## Implemented on-page work

- Homepage title targets `custom workwear supplier china` and `custom uniform supplier china` without presenting LF as an owner of every production facility.
- Each of the five product collections has a unique supplier-led title and buyer-focused meta description.
- The core site positioning prioritises custom workwear, jackets, suits and school uniforms.
- The School, Team & Staff collection targets school-uniform and education-program demand using the existing tracksuit, polo, hoodie, softshell and tailored-product directions.
- `/collections/aviation-flight-suits/` remains a secondary long-tail page with specification-led, claim-safe content. It is linked from the Products collection navigation and relevant industrial content, but is not promoted on the homepage or footer.
- The existing documented 100-piece outerwear project remains the only detailed case study; no client outcome or certification claim has been invented.

## External link acquisition

External backlinks cannot be placed from the website repository. They require access to a customer, partner, directory, association, publication or social account. Use this order of effort:

1. Ask completed-project customers and procurement partners to link to the most relevant LF collection or project page from a supplier, partner or case-study page.
2. Complete legitimate company profiles on industry associations, chambers of commerce and B2B directories using the exact company name, address, email and homepage URL.
3. Publish a short, evidence-backed project summary on the company's LinkedIn and Facebook profiles, linking to `/projects/` rather than repeating generic homepage links.
4. Offer useful specification-led contributions to workwear, PPE, uniform and aviation-supply publications. Link only where the article genuinely supports the reader.
5. Avoid paid link networks, bulk directory submissions, reciprocal-link pages and keyword-stuffed guest posts.

Recommended link targets:

- Homepage for general company profiles.
- `/collections/industrial-workwear/` for workwear supplier references.
- `/collections/protective-hi-vis/` for hi-vis, FR-option and anti-static topics.
- `/collections/team-staff-apparel/` for school-uniform, education and teamwear topics.
- `/collections/aviation-flight-suits/` only for aviation-specific topics.
- `/projects/` when discussing delivery coordination or the documented outerwear example.

## Search Console measurement baseline

Record the deployment date before requesting re-indexing. Compare equivalent 28-day periods rather than day-to-day movement.

Track these page groups separately:

- Homepage
- Five product collections
- Aviation flight-suit landing page
- Product detail pages
- Project example

Track these metrics:

- Indexed status and last crawl date
- Impressions, clicks, click-through rate and average position
- Queries containing `workwear`, `uniform`, `coverall`, `hi vis`, `anti static`, `flight suit` and `aviation`
- Countries generating impressions and qualified inquiries
- Pages with impressions but low click-through rate, which are candidates for title and description testing

Suggested checkpoints:

- Day 0: deploy, submit the sitemap and request indexing for the homepage, four priority product pages, supporting collections, projects page and aviation page.
- Day 7: confirm crawling and index status; do not judge ranking performance yet.
- Day 28: compare the first full 28-day period with the previous 28 days.
- Day 56: keep titles stable unless the data shows a clear mismatch between queries, impressions and click-through rate.

Do not continue changing the homepage during the initial measurement window unless there is a factual, legal or technical error.
