Customization

Built Around Your Logo, Colors and Size Requirements

Selected styles can be customized with printing, embroidery, woven labels, color options, buyer-provided size specifications and custom packaging. Availability and MOQ are confirmed according to the product, decoration method and order quantity.

  • Screen and transfer printing
  • Embroidery and badges

  • Buyer size specifications
  • Fabric and color review

Order workflow

From Product Brief to Delivery Preparation

Each order is reviewed as a project. The final process and checkpoints are adjusted according to product type, customization and buyer requirements.

01

Requirement Review

Confirm category, quantity, logo method, sizes, destination and target schedule.

02

Quotation & Sample

Review suitable styles, material direction and sample requirements before bulk production.

03

Production & QC

Manage production progress and complete quality checks against confirmed specifications.

04

Packing & Delivery

Confirm labeling, packing, export documentation and shipment preparation.
