Custom development

From Standard Logo Apparel to Project-Specific Uniforms

Choose an existing style or request development from a sample, reference image, technical file or size chart. Fabric, construction, colors, branding and packaging are confirmed according to the intended use and order requirements.

  • Custom fabrics, colors and garment construction
  • Printing, embroidery, badges and logo placement
  • Buyer size charts and pattern development
  • Pockets, closures, trims and functional details
  • Private labels, care labels and hangtags
  • Individual, retail-ready or bulk packaging

For garments with functional or protective requirements, material specifications, applicable standards and testing requirements are reviewed separately for each project.
