Production capability

Reliable Production and Order Management

We manage custom apparel orders from sampling and material confirmation through bulk production, inspection, packing and export preparation.

How production is arranged

Structured Support for Project-Based Apparel

Our production process covers pattern making, fabric preparation, cutting, sewing, ironing, inspection and packing for custom apparel and uniform projects.

The exact production setup is selected according to product category, fabric, construction, order quantity and customization requirements. Our team coordinates each project from initial review to delivery preparation.

Production stages

From Technical Review to Packing

Checkpoints are adapted to the product and buyer requirements rather than applied as a one-size-fits-all process.

Product Review

Style, fabric, trims, sizing, logo and packing requirements are clarified.

Pattern & Sample

Pattern preparation, sample development and buyer comments are coordinated.

Material Preparation

Fabric and trims are prepared and reviewed before cutting begins.

Cutting

Layout, spreading and cutting are arranged for the approved production plan.

Sewing

Garment assembly and key workmanship points are followed during production.

Finishing

Pressing, thread cleaning and appearance preparation are completed.

Inspection

Finished garments are checked against the agreed inspection scope.

Packing

Folding, labels, packaging and shipment preparation follow buyer requirements.
