Custom apparel supply

One Team Across Multiple Product Categories

LF Clothing supports corporate and logo apparel, school uniforms, business wear, industrial workwear, team jackets and outdoor clothing for international B2B buyers.

Our team handles requirement review, quotation, product development, sampling, customization, production management, quality control and export preparation.

Buyers have one contact point throughout the project, with product details and commercial requirements confirmed before each order moves forward.

How orders work

A Clear Process From Inquiry to Shipment

Each step is confirmed against the product, quantity, customization and delivery requirements of the project.

01

Requirement Review

Confirm product category, reference style, quantity, sizes, logo method, destination and target schedule.

02

Quotation & Sample

Review material direction, customization details, price and sample requirements before bulk production.

03

Production & Inspection

Follow production progress and check workmanship, measurements, branding, labels and packing requirements.

04

Packing & Export Preparation

Confirm quantity, packing, commercial documents and shipment arrangements under the agreed order terms.

Company and compliance information

Clear Information for Buyer Review

Beijing Lingfeng Apparel Co., Ltd. is the operating company behind LF Clothing. Company registration information can be provided to qualified buyers for commercial due diligence.

Product testing, third-party inspection and market-specific documentation are confirmed according to the product, destination and order requirements rather than presented as blanket certifications.

  • Legal company and contact details
  • Formal quotation and pro forma invoice
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Label and packaging confirmation
  • Inspection or testing coordination when required
  • Export and shipment document preparation
