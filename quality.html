Quality control

Quality Checks for Custom Apparel Orders

Quality requirements are reviewed before production and followed through material preparation, cutting, sewing, finishing and packing according to the product and buyer requirements.

Project-specific control

Clear Requirements Before Inspection

Quality control starts with a clear product specification. Style details, materials, measurements, logo placement, workmanship and packing requirements are reviewed before bulk production.

Inspection scope is then adjusted according to the garment category, order quantity and buyer requirements. Functional performance claims are only applied when the relevant fabric or testing requirement has been confirmed.

Three-stage review

Checks Across the Production Process

The final checklist is agreed according to the order rather than presented as a generic certification.

Stage 01

Before Production

Review approved sample, fabric direction, color, trims, labels, measurements and packing instructions.

Stage 02

During Production

Follow cutting accuracy, logo position, key sewing processes, workmanship and in-process measurements.

Stage 03

Finished Garments

Check appearance, workmanship, measurements, labels, cleanliness and packaging readiness.

Buyer requirements

Additional Inspection Scope

Third-party inspection, testing or market-specific documentation can be discussed when required by the project.

Typical inspection points

What We Pay Attention To

Fabric & Color

Appearance, shade direction and agreed material reference.

Measurements

Key garment measurements checked against the approved size specification.

Workmanship

Seams, stitching, closures, pockets and visible construction details.

Logo Placement

Position, size, color and appearance of printing or embroidery.

Labels

Main labels, size labels, care labels and buyer-provided information.

Finishing

Pressing, thread cleaning, stains and overall garment appearance.

Quantity

Packing quantities and size breakdown checked against order instructions.

Packaging

Folding, bags, cartons, marks and buyer packing requirements.
